## Design Context

### Users
Small product and engineering teams where several people each run a coding agent (Claude Code) against
the same codebase, plus the non-engineers beside them (PMs, designers, ops) who sign in with Google and
use tickets, the roadmap and docs. On the marketing site they are deciding whether to trust a shared
store their agents will read from and write to; in the app they are orienting, reviewing, and confirming.

### Brand Personality
**Calm, trustworthy, precise.** Trov is the team's source of truth, so it should feel like a careful
instrument, not a hype product. Copy is plain-spoken ("Security, in plain terms"). Confidence comes from
showing the real product doing the real thing, not from adjectives.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm paper neutrals (#faf8f3 / #1c1a16), a sage-olive accent (#8a9a5b), Geist + Geist Mono, hairline
borders, generous whitespace. Three themes: Light, Dark, Midnight. Marketing mockups are built from the
app's own tokens and screens, so the site and the product read as one thing.

Motion (chosen 2026-09-19): **alive and product-led**. The mockups act out the product (a diff writes
itself in, a person promotes it; the orient → work → record loop runs), sections reveal on scroll, cards
lift on hover. Quick, confident ease-out (quint/expo); never bounce or elastic; no ambient glows, 3D tilt
or parallax. Everything settles for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Anti-references: neon-on-dark AI palettes, gradient text, glassmorphism, looping decorative motion.

### Design Principles
1. Show, don't claim: every animation demonstrates a real product behavior.
2. Motion confirms, never performs: one orchestrated sequence beats many scattered effects.
3. The human decides: the story always ends with a person's action (Promote), never an agent's.
4. Quiet by default: the page must read fully and calmly with motion off.
